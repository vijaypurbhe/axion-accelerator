# Internal Email: Tech Mahindra Axion Accelerator Launch

## Objective
Draft a brief, crisp internal email to introduce the Tech Mahindra Axion accelerator to sales, delivery, and leadership teams, explain what it does, how to access it, and how to position it with customers.

## Audience
Sales, delivery, and leadership teams across the Salesforce and digital transformation practices.

## Positioning
- **Delivery-led**: Axion is a fast, governed implementation workbench that reduces time-to-value for Salesforce Data 360 and Agentforce programs.
- **AI/Agentforce-led**: It is the trusted, governed data foundation that makes Agentforce and AI agent deployments reliable and enterprise-ready.

## Subject line
Introducing Axion — our Data 360 + Agentforce accelerator

## Email copy

```text
Team,

Today we are introducing **Tech Mahindra Axion**, a production-ready enterprise accelerator built to help clients design, configure, and deploy a trusted, governed, agent-ready data foundation for Salesforce Data 360 and Agentforce.

What Axion is
Axion is both an advisory and readiness platform and an architecture/configuration workbench. It covers the full lifecycle from Discover through Assess, Design, Configure, Validate, Approve, Deploy, Monitor, and Improve.

What Axion does
- Readiness & maturity assessments — understand data, identity, AI, and governance readiness before committing to build.
- Data product blueprinting — define canonical data products, source-to-target mappings, and quality rules.
- Connectivity decision engine — choose the right ingestion pattern (physical, zero-copy, cached, streaming) with scored recommendations.
- Identity resolution studio — design match rules, survivorship policy, and unified profile architecture.
- Trust & governance controls — map regulatory frameworks, evidence controls, risk register, and RACI decision governance.
- Agentforce design workbench — build agent topics, actions, grounding, guardrails, and readiness diagnostics.
- Artifact generation — produce Markdown, traceability, and CSV exports for client delivery and stage-gate approval.

How it helps our customers
Axion lets us move from a slide-led advisory conversation to a live, working proof-point in the same meeting. We can assess a client's readiness, show a target architecture, define data products, simulate identity resolution, and design production-ready Agentforce agents — all in a governed, repeatable workbench. This accelerates deals, de-risks delivery, and demonstrates a clear path to measurable value.

First release: BFSI
The first release is focused on Banking, Financial Services, and Insurance. The platform is architected to extend into HLS and RCPG in subsequent releases.

How to access it
Vijay will share the access link and onboarding flow shortly. The tool is designed for desktop-first use and works best with your Tech Mahindra Google account.

What we need from you
- Try the simulation workspace and provide feedback.
- Use it in customer conversations where a Data 360 or Agentforce opportunity is forming.
- Share wins, gaps, and use cases so we can continue to harden the accelerator.

Questions? Reach out to the Axion product team.

Best regards,
Vijay Purbhe
```
